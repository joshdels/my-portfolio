About Me

Joshua De Leon

LinkedIn GitHub CV

I'm a geospatial developer and GIS specialist who sits between spatial analysis, cartography, and software engineering. I design clear, readable maps and build the systems behind them — turning CAD drawings, land records, and field data into GIS layers, automated pipelines, and interactive WebGIS platforms that teams can run day to day. When a task starts repeating, I automate it, and I use AI as leverage to move faster through the tedious parts.

I work across the full stack of geospatial delivery — Python and Django on the back end, spatial databases for data, and browser-based maps for the people who use them. I'm comfortable in both open-source stacks (QGIS, PostGIS, Leaflet) and enterprise platforms (the ArcGIS ecosystem). I'm open to geospatial developer and GIS roles — Australian teams, remote, or on-site — and happy to walk recruiters and hiring managers through the work behind each project.
