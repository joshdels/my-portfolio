I'm a freelance GIS developer focused on CAD-to-GIS conversion, land parcels, and masterplan drawings to interactive WebGIS. I combine GIS and software development to organize drawing data and make it easier to explore and use.

My focus includes linking parcel geometry to client-provided tax records and preparing map layers for planning teams, with utility layers included where relevant. Each project starts with your source data and defines the checks, deliverables, and documentation your team needs for handover.
