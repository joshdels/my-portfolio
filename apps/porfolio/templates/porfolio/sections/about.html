About Me

Joshua De Leon

LinkedIn GitHub CV

I'm a GIS developer based in Davao, Philippines (UTC+8), with a background in agricultural and biosystems engineering. Most of my work is managing large spatial systems: parcel and field data, the maps made from it, and the databases and tools behind them, so that scattered data becomes something a team can run.

I use Python, Django, and mapping tools alongside QGIS and ArcGIS, and I've built systems that handle millions of parcel records, taken slow manual map work and made it run on its own, and delivered mapping tools that run in the browser. I care about reliable data, work that runs the same way every time, and clear notes on the decisions made. My next step is a remote GIS Developer or Geospatial Developer role, ideally with an Australian or international team that works well with my UTC+8 time zone. I'm also available for small, clearly scoped GIS projects.
