What I do

GIS development that helps a team work from one system.

I'm a GIS developer who helps firms, teams, and organisations bring their scattered spatial data into one organised system that handles large amounts of data, runs the same way every time, and gives people maps and tools they can actually use.

Managing large spatial systems

I set up and look after the system behind an organisation's spatial data, from parcel and field records into the millions, kept organised and quick to search and view in the browser.

Work that runs the same way every time

I take slow, manual map and CAD work and turn it into steps that run on their own, get checked, and are easy for a whole team to trust, hand over, and repeat.

Maps and tools people can use

Mapping and land information tools that run in the browser, so the whole team works from one source instead of scattered files.

Tools and workflow

GIS and spatial data: ArcGIS, QGIS, AutoCAD, and PostGIS. Development and delivery: Python, Django, web mapping, and Docker. Each project page explains the task, my contribution, the checks I performed, and the limitations of the work.
