Experience / How I work

What I do as a geospatial developer.

I turn spatial data into systems teams depend on — from data engineering and GIS analysis through to the WebGIS application and its documentation. Here's the kind of work I take on and how I deliver it.

01 / Data & automation

Spatial data engineering

Convert CAD, parcel, and legacy datasets into clean, aligned GIS layers with structured attributes and documented quality checks. When the work repeats, I automate it in Python and C#/.NET so the pipeline scales.

02 / Analysis & AI

GIS analysis & GeoAI

Spatial analysis for planning, infrastructure, and environmental questions in QGIS and spatial databases — using AI as leverage to speed up repetitive tasks and build GeoAI workflows.

03 / Maps teams use

Cartography & WebGIS

Readable map design and interactive, browser-based platforms built with Django and web mapping libraries — layer control, feature search and inspection, and access management. Open-source (Leaflet, QGIS Server) or enterprise ArcGIS.

End to end, documented and handed over.

I own features from data through deployment: back end, map UI, access, and the usage documentation a team needs to run the system without me. That handover discipline comes from delivering platforms into live local-government use.
