# SEO and publishing guide

## Public positioning

The default identity is **Joshua De Leon — Junior GIS Practitioner**.
The primary audience is an Australian planning, environmental,
surveying, civil, land-development, or geospatial team that can hire a
Philippines-based offshore junior. Small, bounded paid GIS trials are a
secondary path.

Do not optimize the site around a broad freelance service catalogue.
Do not present Joshua as a senior geospatial developer, consultant,
Australian planner, surveyor, cadastral authority, or environmental
approvals specialist.

## Wagtail configuration

1. Set the Wagtail Site hostname and HTTPS port correctly.
2. Review the Home, Projects index, and individual project Promote
   fields. CMS titles and descriptions override source-code defaults
   and may still contain earlier positioning.
3. Keep canonical URLs and the sitemap aligned with published public
   content.
4. Keep filtered or searched project URLs out of the index.

## Project content employers can assess

Each featured project should state:

- the task and intended user;
- data source, licence, date, format, and CRS;
- Joshua's actual contribution;
- the provided requirements or method;
- technical decisions;
- counts, geometry, attribute, transformation, visual, or browser
  checks that actually ran;
- an exception, mistake, or limitation;
- the delivered output;
- reviewer feedback where available; and
- what the project does not prove.

Use Australian open data for new work samples when practical. A
portfolio exercise must be labelled as open-data or synthetic work.
Never expose confidential data, employer tests, access credentials, or
client details without written permission.

## Suggested metadata

Homepage title:

`Joshua De Leon | Junior GIS Practitioner`

Homepage description:

`Joshua De Leon is a Philippines-based junior GIS practitioner seeking offshore work with Australian planning, environmental, surveying, and land-development teams. Review work in mapping, spatial-data preparation, GIS/CAD support, and documented QA.`

Use natural project-specific terms such as QGIS, ArcGIS, cartography,
spatial-data preparation, GIS/CAD support, GDA2020, MGA, WebGIS, or
Python only where the page contains real evidence.

## Measurement

Use Search Console and privacy-appropriate analytics to review:

- Australian impressions and visits;
- searches for Joshua's name and junior GIS terms;
- project-page engagement;
- résumé, LinkedIn, GitHub, and contact actions; and
- qualified role or paid-trial inquiries.

Traffic and rankings are not career validation by themselves. The
useful outcomes are offshore-eligible conversations, interviews, paid
trials, offers, AUD received, and permission-based references.
